How to Load a Rolleiflex Twin-Lens-Reflex Camera

Hi, my name is brandon and I’m both an amateur and professional film maker and photographer based out of the legendary city, Los Angeles. I’ve made my friends at [civic- engagement.net] a nuanced tutorial on how to load a rolleiflex twin-lens-reflex camera. I’ve only ever loaded that camera once before, which is why I had to watch a tutorial on how to unload a medium format camera in the middle of the tutorial. But from the tact that I wield, you can figure why I rack in thousands of dollars a year from making films and photographs.
