August 2, 2026

We have now entered phase 2. Picking things back up and revamping in a crazy way. We have scrapped the older nav bar and gone for a simpler classic blue HTML navigation. It is in the same spot on every page which makes clicking through the pages really exciting and smooth. I like how they look different on each page, giving each section their own feel. When learning how to web develop they really tell you to link everything to the same styling sheet but I think it is much more fun to make different worlds. But for navigation, yes, let's keep that uniform.

I’ve started working on the horizontal gallery scroll. Figured out how to get the scroll bar styled, something I haven’t gotten to do yet so that was fun to learn. I made it blue. Next up I want to figure out a viewport so when you click on the images it will enlarge them.

I took out the old nav bars and I’m keeping the old stuff in the graveyard just in case I want to come back to certain stylings or functions.

Phase 2 is looking up. More soon, Natalie.

Tonight we finalized the home page map as the structure to navigate the site. One of many overarching goals of ours was to try and emulate the experience of exploring in a human built environment, like a city, in a 2d web space. The map gives the website visitor the opportunity to jump into a random corner of the website with no real context of how they ended up there and work their way out through the different layers of digital media. I’m planning to work on the code of a conveyor belt-like gallery landing page where images are generated from a bank and placed on a self moving page in random order, size and position. This will be the initial location for the “Gallery” button containing subsequent links to the vault and theatre. It feels like we are now on the downhill stretch to completion. -Grady
