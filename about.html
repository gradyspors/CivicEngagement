Civic Engagement is an organization dedicated to shared artistic practice for community improvement. Some of our goals include becoming a resource for essential goods, an outlet for diverse information, and hub for creative language. We are based in Seattle, WA and Los Angeles, CA.

Who Are We?

Natalie Rosenstrom is a visual designer and graduate of Cornish College of the Arts. Operating at the intersection of graphic, object and web design, their style is fueled by a love for color and pattern.

Grady Spors is a multidisciplinary audio professional, music producer and pianist. He aspires to reach a deep understanding of the recording arts and its impact on contemporary culture.

Solomon Kruger is a music producer and writer, interested in linguistics and history. Their philanthropic sensibilities are revealed through swaths of relatable melodies and innovative storytelling.
